# The story

**Spoilers.** This is the whole story of the game, the ending included.

It was written on 12 November 2026, before any gameplay was built, because Eshan stopped the build and asked for it: "We need everything to happen for a meaning, flashbacks, backstory, reason behind every action we make the player do ... and the sudden feeling of everything clicking at the very end of the game. Build everything around it."

**Status: approved by Eshan on 13 November 2026.** Asked "Is this the story the game gets built around?", he answered "Yes, build it". So it is fixed. Nothing in the game may contradict it, and nothing goes into the game that it does not need. Changing it takes his say-so.

He has not yet decided the five things listed at the bottom: the names, the title, how the two of them text, and the setting. The working versions in this file stand until he does. The wording of the task lines and the messages can still be polished, as long as what they mean stays the same.

His five beats are all still here, in his order: the flat, the chores, the phone, the edit, the payoff.

---

## The story in one minute

**What the player is told.** You are a freelance video editor, alone in a flat that is too big for one person. It is late. You do the last chores of the day. A number you do not know offers far too much money for a small job: three clips, something goes wrong in each one, find the frame and mark it. The clips are of empty rooms. Every time you scrub back, they have changed, and they look more and more like your own flat. Then someone in the footage notices you.

**What is really going on.** You died in this flat a year ago tonight, at 3:07 in the morning. You have repeated your last night ever since without knowing it. The chores at the start of the game are the haunting: a rubbish bag that crosses an empty corridor by itself, a bolt that slides shut in an empty flat, a gas ring that lights itself. The footage is real. It was filmed in your flat after it was cleared out, by the one person who cannot let you go. He is the unknown number. Scrubbing back rewinds the tape past its own first frame, to the night you died.

**The part that should hurt.** The player did it themselves. In the first three minutes they bolt the front door and walk away from a pan of chai on a lit ring, with their own hands, because the game asked them to. Those are the two things that killed him.

**The idea underneath.** A haunting is a recording that keeps playing. He is an editor. Tonight somebody finally sends him the footage, and he has to find the cut.

---

## Who

- **Saad** (working name). The player. A freelance video editor. Never seen and never heard.
- **Hamza** (working name). His oldest friend. They shared this flat and ran a two-man video business out of the study: Hamza shot, Saad cut. The spare room was Hamza's.

Setting: a city in Pakistan, never named. November, cold, every window shut.

## What really happened, in order

1. **Two friends.** They rent the flat after university and work from it for four years. The film festival poster and the certificate in the study are from the short film they made together. So is the poster over the sofa: a figure standing in a lit doorway.
2. **The fight.** Hamza takes a salaried job abroad. Saad takes it as being left behind. They fight in the corridor. Hamza goes, and never comes back for his last boxes. Saad deletes his number, stays on in a flat too big for one, and takes every job he can get to cover both halves of the rent.
3. **The night: 16 November 2026.** The date is ringed on the kitchen calendar. It is a deadline.
   - 00:40. He takes out the rubbish and bolts the front door.
   - 01:00. He puts a pan of chai on the hob to stay awake.
   - 01:04. His phone buzzes in the study. A number he does not know: "Are you still up?" Then: "It's Hamza. New number." He reads it. He does not answer. He sits down at the desk, puts his headphones on, and falls asleep.
   - 01:10. The chai boils over and puts out the flame. The gas keeps coming.
   - 02:31. "I know you've seen these. I'm coming over."
   - 03:07. Hamza is at the door. His old key turns the lock, but the bolt is on. He hammers on the door. He rings the landline and can hear it ringing inside. He smells gas. Saad dies at 3:07, asleep, with his friend hammering on his door.
4. **The year after.** The flat is cleared out. Hamza comes home for good and cannot let the flat go, so he pays the rent on it, empty. He texts Saad's number every night at 3:07: "Are you still up?" The woman downstairs hears someone walking about up there. He finds the bolt on, from the inside. He finds a ring lit. He is a cameraman, so he does the one thing he knows how to do: he puts a camera on a tripod and films the empty rooms at night.
5. **Tonight: 16 November 2027.** One year on. Hamza sits in the empty flat with his footage and sends the message he sends every night. For the first time, an answer comes back: "Who is this?" He does not dare say. The last time he said who he was, he got silence. So he offers the one thing Saad never turned down: a job.

What Hamza needs from the job is one answer. For a year he has believed that if he had come sooner, or broken the door down faster, Saad would be alive. The frames Saad marks tell him the truth: the bolt went on at 00:52 and the flame went out at 01:10, two hours before he knocked and long before he had even set off. It was the chai. It was not him.

## The rules

These hold everywhere in the game. Anything new has to obey them.

1. **The flat you walk through is the night Saad remembers.** It is always 3:07 there.
2. **The tape shows what is really there.** The camera is always right.
3. **Scrubbing forward plays the tape. Scrubbing back takes it further back than it should go:** past its own first frame, through the year, to the night.
4. **Saad cannot leave the flat, has no reflection, and is never seen or heard.** The one exception is the last frame of the last clip. (This is also why the game has one location, fake mirrors and no voices. The limits of the build are the rules of the story.)
5. **Hamza is alive, and there is no monster.** Nobody is trying to hurt anybody. Everything frightening is either Hamza, or that night coming back.
6. **Nothing before the ending says in words that Saad is dead.**

## The game, beat by beat

About 14 minutes. The words in quotes are what the task line says.

### Part 1. An ordinary night (about 3 minutes)

The flat feels safe. That is the job of this part, and the trap.

1. **Start.** The study, behind the desk chair. Every clock in the flat says 3:07. "Take out the rubbish."
2. **The rubbish.** Pick up the full bag by the kitchen bin, carry it down the corridor, put it out. The front door opens a crack. The landing outside is pitch black. The door shuts.
3. **The bolt.** "Lock up." Slide the bolt on the front door.
4. **The chai.** "Make chai." Light the ring under the pan. "Wait for it to boil."
5. **The phone.** A few seconds later his phone buzzes in the study, loud enough to hear from the kitchen. "Your phone is buzzing in the study." The chai line is the only one in the game that never gets ticked off. The player walks away from a lit ring. The game does not mention it again until the end.

### Part 2. The job (about 8 minutes)

6. **The unknown number.** Pick up the phone. A short exchange (the script is below). The offer is too good and the client knows too much. Three files arrive. "Sit down at the PC."
7. **How the edit works.** Each clip is a locked-off shot of an empty room at night, with a date and time burnt into the corner. Something has moved by the end of it, but the moment it moves is lost in a burst of tape static. So the player has to scrub back to find it, and every time they scrub back the room has more of Saad's things in it, and the date in the corner is earlier. When the moment is on screen, they mark it.
8. **Clip 1: the corridor.** Dated August 2027: next year. A bare corridor that could be anywhere. A black rubbish bag is by the far door at the end, and was not at the start. Scrub back: a doormat, a shoe rack, pictures on the wall, a bulb that flickers. It is his corridor. The bag slides down it with nobody carrying it. Mark it. On one pass there is a figure standing at the far end, in the dark by the front door. On the next it is gone.
9. **Clip 2: the front door.** The bolt is shut at the end and open at the start. Scrub back: the split wood round the lock is whole again. The bolt slides across by itself. Mark it. Then the tape jumps on to 3:07 and something hammers on the door from outside, hard enough to shake it.
10. **The door, for real.** The same hammering, from his own front door, down the corridor behind him. This is the loud scare. The player gets up and walks the corridor. Through the peephole: an empty, black landing. Nobody.
11. **Clip 3: the kitchen.** A ring on the hob lights by itself. Scrub back: a pan is on it. The chai rises, boils over, and the flame goes out. The knob is still turned on. Mark the frame where the flame dies. From now on there is a faint hiss in the real kitchen.
12. **One more file.** "There is one more. I did not want to send it."
13. **Clip 4: the study.** An empty room. Scrub back: a desk, a monitor, a chair. It is this room. The time in the corner is tonight's, and it is running: this is live. Far off, through two doorways, someone is sitting in the chair in the spare room. Each time the player scrubs back he is nearer: the spare room doorway, the corridor, the study doorway, right beside the desk, bending to look at the screen. He has noticed. On the monitor in the footage the timeline is moving. The chair in front of it is empty. If the player turns round in the real room, nobody is there.
14. **All the way back.** The date falls back to 16 November 2026, 3:07. Somebody is asleep at the desk in a grey hoodie, headphones on, head on his arms. It is the only time the player sees him.

### Part 3. The cut (about 3 minutes)

15. **The thread.** The phone lights up. The player has only ever seen tonight's messages. Now the thread scrolls up through a year of them, every one sent at 3:07, back to the night itself and the name. The script is below. This is where it clicks: each line is one of the chores, seen from the other side of the door.
16. **The gas.** "Turn off the gas." In the kitchen the flame is out, the chai has boiled over, and the hiss is loud. Turn the knob. Silence. The clock on the microwave changes to 3:08.
17. **The door.** "Open the door." The corridor bulb has stopped flickering. Slide the bolt back. The door opens. The landing light is on.
18. **The last shot.** On tape. The empty flat, 16 November 2027. The front door stands open with light coming through it, and Hamza stands in the corridor looking at it: a figure in a lit doorway, the same picture as the poster of their film. The time in the corner turns from 3:07 to 3:08. STOP. The title. Credits.

## The scares, and what each one is

The player should spend Part 2 believing a stranger has been inside their home and is in it now. Every scare is true to that fear, and true to the ending.

1. Footage that changes when you scrub back.
2. The footage is of your own flat. Someone has been in here with a camera.
3. A figure at the end of the corridor, on the tape.
4. Hammering on the front door at 3 a.m., and nobody there. (Hamza, a year ago.)
5. The figure in the live clip, nearer every time you look, until he is where you are sitting. (Hamza, tonight, realising his friend is in the room.)
6. The empty chair.
7. The hiss.

He has been afraid, all night, of the only person who stayed.

## What "flashbacks" means in this game

Three kinds, and none of them is a cutscene.

- **Seen.** Scrubbing back on the tape rewinds each room through the year, to the night. The central mechanic is the flashback.
- **Read.** The message thread at the end: the night itself, minute by minute, and then the year after it.
- **Heard.** The hammering and the landline are that night, breaking into this one.

What is not in: flashback scenes with people moving and talking. That needs character models, animation and voices, and there are four days.

**Must be in, or the story does not work:** the three chores; the phone; the editing screen with all four clips, each in two states (emptied and furnished); the figure, standing still in a few places; the hammering; the thread; the gas; the door; the last shot.

**Cut first if a day is lost,** in this order: the figure in clip 1; a half-way state for each room (boxes and dust sheets); the landline ringing between clips; the bag being found back inside the door.

**Dropped from the old outline:** the pool at night. Every clip has to be this flat or the ending does not click, and a pool is a second location that would take a day to build.

**Still out:** voices, faces, people who move, a second ending, anything outside the flat.

## What only Eshan can decide

The first is answered: yes, on 13 November. The other four are still open.

1. **Is this the story?** In particular the ending: he has been dead for a year, and the stranger in the footage is his friend. "He was dead all along" has been done in films. What is ours is how you find out: you did the haunting yourself in the first three minutes, and the game showed it back to you as somebody else's footage.
2. **The names.** Saad and Hamza are placeholders.
3. **The title.** The story offers three. "Still Up?" is the first message of the night, the message Hamza sends every night for a year, and what a ghost is. "3:07" is the clue that is on every clock. "Unknown Number" is the plainest.
4. **How they text.** Plain English with a word of Urdu here and there ("yaar"), or more than that.
5. **The setting.** A city in Pakistan that is never named. Say if it should be somewhere else.
